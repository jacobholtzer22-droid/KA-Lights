{config.displayName} installs fresh mulch in landscape beds across {config.primaryCity}, including bed edging and weed removal, so the finished beds look sharp and stay that way through the season.

## What happens on installation day?

We start by redefining the bed edges with a clean spade cut, pull weeds and clear debris, then spread mulch to a consistent two to three inch depth. Plant stems and tree trunks are kept clear so moisture does not sit against them. Walkways and the driveway are blown clean before we leave.

## How is mulch installation quoted?

Price depends on the square footage of the beds, the mulch you choose, and how much prep the beds need. We measure on site and send a written quote by the cubic yard, so you can see exactly how the number was reached before we order material.

## Which mulch should I choose?

Natural hardwood breaks down into the soil and suits most planting beds. Dyed brown or black mulch holds its color longer and reads cleaner against a house. We will talk through the trade-offs when we measure, and you can [ask us](/contact) for samples.
