## What are permanent Christmas and holiday lights?

Permanent holiday lighting, also called permanent Christmas lights or permanent architectural lighting, is a string of LED lights mounted along your roofline that stays up all year instead of being hung and taken down each season. {service.shortDescription}

Because the same lights handle every occasion, one system covers Christmas, Halloween, the Fourth of July, game days, and a clean warm white on an ordinary night. You can preview the scenes in the [color visualizer on the homepage](/).

## How are permanent lights mounted?

The lights sit in a track installed along the eaves of your roofline. The track runs the lines of the house, following eaves and gables, so the lights trace the shape of the roof. You can see the steps from request to install on [how permanent lighting installation works](/how-it-works).

## Can you see permanent lights during the day?

The track and lights stay on the house all year, so they are there in daylight. How much they stand out depends on where they mount along your roofline and how the track sits against your eaves and trim. If daytime appearance matters to you, ask about it when you request a quote.

## How do you control permanent lights?

The system is app-controlled. You tap a scene on your phone and the whole home changes: warm white for everyday, full color for holidays, game days, or any occasion.

## What affects the cost of permanent lighting?

Pricing is {service.priceNote}. In practice that means the length of roofline you want lit and how involved the installation is, for example how many stories and gables the run covers. Every roofline is different, so there is no single price; a quote for your home is the way to get a real number.

## Do you need HOA approval for permanent lights?

It depends on your HOA. Many homeowner associations have rules about exterior lighting or changes to the outside of a home. Check your HOA's guidelines, or ask the board, before you schedule an install.

## Permanent vs. seasonal Christmas lights: what is the difference?

Seasonal lights go up before the holidays and come down after, every year. A permanent system is mounted once along the roofline and stays up, and because it is app-controlled, the same lights can change from holiday colors to warm white without anyone getting on a ladder. More answers are on the [permanent lighting FAQ](/faq).
