## What does a fall cleanup include?

Leaves are removed from the lawn, beds, and hard surfaces, perennials and ornamental grasses are cut back, and gutters at ground level are checked for buildup if you ask. Everything is loaded and hauled off the same day, so there are no bags waiting at the curb.

## What does a spring cleanup include?

Winter debris and matted leaves are cleared from the lawn and beds, bed edges are recut, and the first mow of the season can be added on. Spring is also when we flag anything that did not survive the winter so you can decide what to replace.
